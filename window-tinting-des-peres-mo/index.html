Why window film fits Des Peres homes and businesses

Des Peres incorporated in 1934, but most of its neighborhoods came later. According to the city's comprehensive plan, the population nearly tripled between 1950 and 1960 as subdivisions like Harwood Hills, Winslow, Elaine Acres, St. Clement Acres, Andre Hills, Bayberry Hills and Ballas Meadows filled in, followed by Saddle Spur and Berkley Manor in the early 1960s. Building continued through the 1970s and 1980s, so today the housing mix is largely ranches, split-levels and two-story homes from that post-war era.

Homes from those decades tend to share a few traits. Living rooms often have a large picture window facing the street. Rear family rooms and additions frequently open to the yard through sliding patio doors. Many houses still have some original or older replacement windows, which let more heat through in summer and feel cold in winter compared with newer glass. Window film is a way to improve comfort, privacy and UV protection in those rooms without replacing windows.

On the commercial side, Des Peres sits at the junction of I-270 and Manchester Road. West County Center, which opened in 1969, anchors a retail corridor of shops, restaurants and offices along Manchester Road, and Edward Jones has its headquarters in the city. Storefront glass along a busy east-west road takes strong morning or afternoon sun depending on which side of the street it faces.

Window film options for Des Peres homes

Heat control for picture windows and patio doors

The most common residential window tinting request in a ranch or split-level is a room that runs hot every afternoon. Solar heat control window film rejects part of the sun's heat and reduces glare on west- and south-facing glass. Performance varies by film, so we show you the spec sheet (TSER, VLT and glare reduction) before you choose. For an honest look at what to expect on your utility bills, read does window film lower energy bills.

Privacy for street-facing glass

Many Des Peres ranches and split-levels have a large front picture window that looks straight out to the street. Privacy window film can cut the view in from the street while keeping natural light. Reflective films work during the day; for privacy after dark, a frosted or patterned film is the more reliable choice. Our guide to the best window film for daytime and nighttime privacy explains the difference.

Frosted and decorative film for bathrooms and entries

Bathroom windows, front-door sidelights and the glass panels common on homes of this era are good candidates for decorative and frosted window film. It gives the look of etched or obscure glass without replacing the pane, and it can be removed later if your needs change.

UV protection for floors and furniture

Refinished hardwood is a popular update in older Des Peres homes, and sunny rooms can fade it unevenly. Most quality films block up to 99% of UV. If fading is your main concern, a nearly clear UV and fade protection film protects the room without darkening it.

Commercial window tinting in Des Peres

For retail, restaurant, office and medical tenants along Manchester Road and near the I-270 interchange, commercial window tinting helps with hot seating areas near the glass, glare on screens and registers, and faded window displays. Privacy and frosted films are also useful for offices and exam rooms that face parking lots. If your space is in a shopping center, check with your landlord or property manager about rules on exterior appearance before choosing a reflective film.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you put film on the original windows in an older Des Peres home?

Usually, yes. Many Des Peres homes date to the 1950s through the 1980s and some still have older single-pane or early double-pane windows. We check the glass type and condition during the estimate, because the right film depends on it, and we will tell you if a window is not a good candidate.

What is the best privacy option for a picture window facing the street?

It depends on whether you need privacy during the day, at night, or both. Reflective films give daytime privacy but reverse at night when lights are on inside, while frosted or decorative films give privacy around the clock. We bring samples so you can see each option on your own window.

Do you tint storefronts near West County Center and Manchester Road?

Yes. We install commercial window film on retail, restaurant, office and medical glass in Des Peres, including along the Manchester Road corridor. If your building is in a shopping center, check with your landlord or property manager about any rules on exterior appearance first.

Will window film lower my energy bills?

It can help, mainly by cutting solar heat through sunny windows in summer and, with low-e film, holding in some heat in winter. Results vary by film, window orientation and the home, so we do not promise a specific savings number. We show you each film's spec sheet so you can compare.

How long does a typical home installation take?

Many home projects can be finished in a day, depending on how many windows are filmed and how easy they are to reach. Film needs some cure time afterward, and we leave care instructions so you know when you can clean the windows.
